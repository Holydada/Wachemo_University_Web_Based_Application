Wachemo University Landing Page

How to run:
1) Put 'wcu-landing.html' and your 'logo2.png' in the same folder.
2) Double-click 'wcu-landing.html' to open in your browser.
3) (Optional) Edit the CSS variable --hero-image in the <style> section to set a background image, e.g.:
   --hero-image: url('hero.jpg');
