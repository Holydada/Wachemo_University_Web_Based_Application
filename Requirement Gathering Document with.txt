       *Library Management Function(
            -There are different libraries in our university, such as Health, Technology, Computational, Fresh, and Social libraries. The process works like this:

A student comes to a library to read notes or other materials.
If the student wants to use a physical book, they give their digital ID to the storekeeper.
The storekeeper writes the student’s name and the book title on a paper and gives the book to the student.
After the student finishes reading, they return the book to the storekeeper.
The storekeeper manually searches for the student’s ID in the list, checks the name and book details, then removes the entry from the paper.
Finally, the storekeeper returns the student’s ID.

-so in this part to make this system best and powerful. we must fetch students information with their college from some module to this library management system.
